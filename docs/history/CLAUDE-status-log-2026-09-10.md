**Manual in-app punch (2026-09-24):** while playback rolls, RECORD punches at the live playhead even if an older EDIT cursor exists; when stopped, an explicitly placed cursor is used. A manual punch ends with one RECORD or STOP press, while a normal live take retains the two-press safety guard. The punch writer must adopt the existing take's container/bit depth on primary, backup and mirrors before stashing any file; mixed/unreadable existing media must fail closed. Keep originals as `.punchbase` until every spliced copy commits, surface rollback, and save the local session document/mix on stop. Continuation at the end remains a separate part file, not a punch into the original.

**Punch recovery/alignment (2026-09-24):** never delete an existing `.punchbase` to start a new pass. Recover it before session playback/open, archiving any partial replacement under that session's `Session File Backups/Interrupted Punch*`; refuse a new capture when recovery occurred until the restored take is reopened. All copies must have a matching original take before a punch; complete all stashes before opening writers. Pre-roll history applies to fresh takes only. Newly armed tracks in a punch/continue need a silent lead-in to preserve their timeline alignment. Selection punches capture only `[in,out)` in the audio callback, not from the UI timer; loop playback is suspended until teardown, and monitoring switches the armed track from old take to live input only during capture. More-than-stereo imports split every channel into a mono track and roll back the whole source on failure.
